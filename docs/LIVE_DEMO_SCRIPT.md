# WorkProof — Live Demo Script (3:30 target)

A judge-facing product demo for screen-recording the running app. Time-coded to land at **3:30**, well under the 4:00 cap. ~150 wpm at a natural pace.

**Before you record:**
- Pre-seed one or two example claims so *Discover* isn't empty.
- Open Chrome at the home page, zoom 110%, hide bookmarks bar.
- macOS Do Not Disturb on. Replace `[YOUR NAME]`.

---

## [0:00 – 0:20]  Hook
*(Home page hero. Don't click.)*

> "I'm [YOUR NAME], and this is **WorkProof** — employment verification powered by zero-knowledge proofs and AI on the Midnight Network. Every résumé you've ever read is built on trust — self-reported claims, an unverified LinkedIn, a reference call no one ever makes. WorkProof fixes that — without leaking any of the candidate's private data."

---

## [0:20 – 0:40]  How it works
*(Slow scroll down the home page to the protocol section.)*

> "Three roles. A **candidate** commits a claim — and their identity is cryptographically hidden the moment they submit. A **verifier** confirms a credibility signal. A **recruiter** sees an AI trust score and the signals behind it — but never any raw personal data. One shared truth, zero blind trust."

---

## [0:40 – 1:10]  Step 1 — Submit a claim
*(Click "My Claims". Type the values while you narrate.)*

> "I'm the candidate. I open *My Claims* and enter my email, the company — Deloitte — my role, and the dates I worked there. Nothing private has been sent yet. The app hashes my email and the company name in the browser before anything leaves the page."

---

## [1:10 – 1:25]  Step 2 — Submit, the proof is generated
*(Click Submit. Wait for the result card.)*

> "I hit submit. A zero-knowledge proof is generated right here, committing the claim cryptographically — without exposing my identity or the exact duration on chain."

---

## [1:25 – 2:10]  Step 3 — The AI trust score appears  *(this is the headline)*
*(Stay on the result card. Let viewers read the score and tags.)*

> "And here's where the AI takes over. The app returns a trust score from zero to one hundred and a list of explainability tags telling me **exactly why** the score is what it is. Right now it's low — and that's correct. I just self-claimed this; nobody has confirmed it. The model is honest about what's missing — *email domain unverified*, *LinkedIn not provided*, *no peer endorsements*. No black box.
>
> The scorer combines five signal channels — email-domain match, LinkedIn consistency, duration realism, peer endorsements, and adversarial-pattern detection. It's deterministic, privacy-preserving — it only ever sees hashed data — and anti-gameable. Suspicious patterns *lower* the score; you can't pump it up by spamming claims."

---

## [2:10 – 2:40]  Step 4 — Verify, watch the AI react live
*(Click Verify. Paste the Claim ID. Pick "Email Domain". Type `deloitte.com`. Submit.)*

> "Now I switch hats. I'm an HR verifier at Deloitte. I open *Verify*, look up the claim, pick *email domain*, and submit. Watch the screen — the audit log updates in real time, the trust score moves up by thirty points, and the *email domain unverified* tag drops off the list. The credibility model is updating live, signal by signal."

---

## [2:40 – 3:05]  Step 5 — Recruiter view
*(Click Discover. Scroll the proven-claims list.)*

> "Final hat: the recruiter. I open *Discover*. Recruiters never log in as the candidate — they only inspect. Every row shows the trust score, the confidence band, and the verification signals behind it. But notice what's *not* here: no email, no real company name, no résumé. Privacy and trust at the same time."

---

## [3:05 – 3:30]  Why this matters & close
*(Cut back to the home page. Hold the final frame.)*

> "Three takeaways. One — this is a real, working product. Two — the AI is the differentiator: explainable, deterministic, privacy-preserving. Three — Midnight is the right substrate: the chain proves *what happened*, the AI scores *how much to trust it*. WorkProof is fully working today. I'm [YOUR NAME] — thanks for watching, questions next."

*(Stop recording at 3:30.)*

## Recording tips

- Record audio in one clean take with **QuickTime → File → New Audio Recording**, then layer the screen capture on top in iMovie / CapCut.
- During Step 1 type at a natural pace — the typing *is* the proof this is a real demo.
- Inflect on the words *prove*, *without*, *explainable*, *live*, *trust*.
- If you stumble, keep going — edit the gap out, don't restart the take.
- Final export: **1080p, H.264, MP4, ≤ 200 MB.**

## If you finish early

- Hold the final home-page frame in silence for the remaining seconds — clean ending beats rushed words.

## If you run long

- Trim the second paragraph of Step 3 (the five signal channels) down to one line: *"It's a multi-signal scorer that's explainable, deterministic, and privacy-preserving — it only ever sees hashed data."*
